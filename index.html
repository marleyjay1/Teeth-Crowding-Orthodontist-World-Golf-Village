What Is Teeth Crowding?

Teeth crowding occurs when the available space in the jaw is insufficient for the teeth to develop into their ideal positions. Instead of sitting evenly beside one another, teeth may overlap or become rotated.

Crowding can develop for several reasons, including:

  • Limited space in the dental arch
  • Jaw development and growth patterns
  • Tooth size compared with available jaw space
  • Early or late loss of baby teeth
  • Teeth shifting over time
  • Changes that occur after previous orthodontic treatment

Crowded teeth are not only a cosmetic concern. Overlapping teeth can make brushing and flossing more difficult, which may make maintaining good oral hygiene more challenging.

How Orthodontic Treatment Can Correct Crowding

The goal of orthodontic treatment is to gradually guide teeth toward healthier positions while improving the relationship between the upper and lower teeth.

Depending on the severity and cause of crowding, an orthodontist may recommend braces, clear aligners, or another orthodontic approach.

Traditional Braces

Traditional braces use brackets and wires to apply controlled pressure to the teeth. Over time, this pressure helps move teeth into better alignment.

Braces can be used for a wide range of orthodontic problems, including crowded teeth and bite irregularities.

Invisalign and Clear Aligners

Clear aligners provide a more discreet alternative for appropriate cases. The removable trays are designed to gradually move teeth according to a customized treatment plan.

Whether clear aligners are appropriate depends on the patient's individual orthodontic needs. An orthodontic consultation is the best way to determine which treatment option may provide the desired results.

Signs You May Need Treatment for Crowded Teeth

You may benefit from an orthodontic evaluation if you notice:

  • Teeth overlapping one another
  • Rotated or twisted teeth
  • Difficulty flossing between certain teeth
  • Crooked front teeth
  • Teeth that appear pushed forward or backward
  • Difficulty biting comfortably
  • Changes in your bite
  • Teeth becoming more crowded over time

Children can also benefit from an early orthodontic evaluation because developing alignment concerns may be easier to identify while the mouth and jaws are still growing.
